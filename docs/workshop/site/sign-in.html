Sign in to the CSOC

Sign in to the CSOC portal as the administrator. From here on, you manage Gen3 from the CSOC.

Wizard step DoneTime about 1 minute

Goal

Sign in to the CSOC portal through its Keycloak as the user admin.

Steps

  1. On the Done step, press Open the CSOC, or open https://portal.csoc.example.org.
  2. Browser The portal sends you to Keycloak's sign-in page for the realm csoc-realm, which shows its name as "CSOC-REALM". Enter the username admin and the password you saved, and press Sign In.
  3. If the page does not load (403 Forbidden, or a refused connection), you are on a network that is not in the allowed addresses, for example a VPN. Add it with Allow a new IP address.

Done when: the CSOC portal opens, signed in as admin, with its setup wizard Welcome to CSOC and "Connected to AWS".

Expected result

The CSOC portal opens. "Connected to AWS" means the portal reached its cluster's Kubernetes API and found that the cluster's nodes run on AWS. A sign-in to the CSOC realm lasts up to 12 hours, and ends after 8 hours without activity.

What's happening

The load balancer admits your browser because your address is in the allowed addresses. The portal has no session for you yet, so it sends you to Keycloak. After you sign in, Keycloak returns you to the portal, which confirms the sign-in with Keycloak directly; that call leaves the cluster through its outbound address, which is why that address is allowed too. The portal reaches AWS with its own IAM role and reads the same state bucket as the wizard, so it already knows this environment's history.

To let people sign in with their institutional accounts, see Connect your institution's sign-in. For the flow in detail, see Sign-in.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
NXDOMAIN, or "This site can't be reached"Your resolver remembers an earlier "no such name" answer.Run dig on the hostname; if it returns an address, switch your computer to a public resolver such as 1.1.1.1.
403 Forbidden or a refused connectionYour current address is not in the allowed addresses, for example after joining a VPN.See Allow a new IP address.
Keycloak asks for your email, first and last nameAdmin email (optional) was left empty in the bootstrap.Fill them in once.
Sign-in loops, or fails after KeycloakThe cluster's outbound address changed, for example after a Squid instance was replaced, so the portal cannot reach Keycloak.Open Advanced on the Install step, press Bootstrap, then Finish install. The bootstrap detects the new address.
You do not know the admin passwordThe wizard does not store it.See Reset the admin password.
